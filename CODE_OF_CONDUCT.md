# Code of conduct

Clipnest is a small project, and we want everyone who uses or works on it to
feel welcome.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Give feedback on the work, not on the person.
- Respect people's time: keep issues and reviews focused and clear.

## What is not okay

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory language or jokes of any kind.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Continuing to contact someone after they asked you to stop.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, contact the maintainers privately through
https://erag.in. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove
comments, close issues or block people who break these rules.

This code of conduct is inspired by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
